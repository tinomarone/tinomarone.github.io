Research

Private equity and hospital acquisitions

Research assistant · Prof. Ryan McDevitt · 2026–present

Linking Medicare ownership disclosures to a hand-built list of hospital transactions, and working out which apparent ownership changes are real events and which are artifacts of how the filings were processed.

Two thirds of deals have no counterpart in the enrollment data. Most of that gap is scope mismatch rather than underreporting, but not all of it.

Medicare durable medical equipment fraud

Research assistant · NBER working paper · 2026–present

Turning enforcement documents into a structured panel, resolving supplier identifiers across sources, and scoring how much each piece of evidence should be trusted depending on whether it was court-verified or circumstantial.

Extraction from unstructured text is never fully reliable. The confidence score exists so downstream users can see where the uncertainty sits instead of inheriting it silently.

Market outcomes in non-competitive-bidding areas

Independent seminar paper · 2026

An analysis of Medicare DMEPOS markets outside the competitive bidding program, asking what happens to prices and supplier participation in areas the program never reached.

Every state was treated at once, so there is no clean control group. The paper argues that this limits what can be identified, and says so rather than working around it.

Value factor construction

In progress · 2026

Rebuilding the standard and timely-price versions of the HML value factor from public data, to see for myself how a lagged-price convention changes the factor's correlation with momentum.

A replication is only as good as its handling of look-ahead bias. Notes on what I got wrong first will go in the repository alongside the code.
